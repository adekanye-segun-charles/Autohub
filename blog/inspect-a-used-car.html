How to Inspect a Used Car Before You Buy

Buying Guide · September 18, 2026 · 6 min read

A quick checklist to help you check a used car before placing an order.

Buying a pre-owned car is a smart way to get more for your money. Use this checklist to review the car and its paperwork before placing an order.

1. Check the paperwork

Ask for the service history, ownership documents and customs papers. Check that the chassis number on the documents matches the number on the car.

2. Inspect the body

  • Look along each panel in daylight for ripples or mismatched paint.
  • Check panel gaps are even on both sides.
  • Inspect tyres for uneven wear.

3. Check the interior and engine

Test the air-conditioning and electrical features. Look for damp carpets, leaks, cracked hoses and low fluids.

4. Confirm the details
